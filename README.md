# 🩺 MedAce AI – AI-Powered MBBS Study Companion

> An AI-powered study assistant designed specifically for MBBS students to simplify learning, generate exam-focused study material, and improve revision efficiency.

---

## 📖 Overview

MedAce AI is a web application built to help medical students prepare smarter for professional examinations. Instead of searching through multiple textbooks and websites, students can simply enter a topic and instantly receive structured, AI-generated study material.

The application generates concise summaries, viva questions, MCQs, revision notes, mnemonics, clinical correlations, and rapid revision plans tailored to the student's subject and MBBS year.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Medical students often spend a significant amount of time gathering information from multiple sources before they can start studying.

**MedAce AI solves this problem by providing:**

- Instant AI-generated study material
- High-yield exam preparation
- Quick revision notes
- Interactive learning experience
- A single platform for multiple study needs

## 🤖 AI Feature

MedAce AI uses **OpenAI GPT-5.5** (through the Lovable AI Gateway) to generate personalized medical study material.

Users select:

- Subject
- MBBS Year
- Study Mode
- Topic

The AI then produces structured, exam-oriented content in the selected format.

### Study Modes

- Summary
- MCQs
- Viva Questions
- Revision Notes
- Simplify Topic
- Clinical Correlation
- Mnemonics
- Rapid Revision
